## Confirmed care areas

These seven areas come from S1. The original source repeats the number 6; the numbering below identifies its seven distinct areas without adding a service. English copy must preserve the same clinical scope and qualifications.

### 1. Dental surgery and complex extractions

French: *Chirurgie dentaire et extractions complexes*.

- Simple and complex dental extractions.
- Removal of impacted or partially impacted wisdom teeth.
- Surgical adjuncts to orthodontic treatment: *cerclage*, bone anchorage, corticotomies and frenectomies.
- Apical surgery and apical resections.

These surgical adjuncts do not establish that Dr Di Donna provides full orthodontic treatment, braces or aligners, or holds an orthodontic specialist qualification. Do not expand *cerclage* into an unconfirmed technique or treatment promise.

### 2. Oral implantology and bone reconstruction

French: *Implantologie orale et reconstruction osseuse*.

- Dental implant placement.
- Extraction with immediate implant placement **when clinical conditions permit**.
- Surgical treatment of peri-implantitis.
- Bone grafting, including horizontal and vertical bone augmentation.
- Sinus floor elevation (*sinus lift*).

Preserve the conditional indication for immediate placement. Do not infer immediate loading, same-day teeth, a complete prosthetic offer, a particular implant or graft material, or a fixed healing timeline.

### 3. Stomatology and oral medicine

French: *Stomatologie et médecine buccale*.

Assessment and treatment of changes or lesions affecting the oral mucosa, which may range from common ulcers to more serious lesions.

- Diagnostic biopsies.
- Surgical excision of **certain** lesions.
- Monitoring of chronic mucosal conditions.

Keep the limitation to selected lesions. Do not imply that every lesion is treated surgically, that all cancers are treated at the cabinet, or that a diagnosis or cure is guaranteed.

### 4. Soft tissue surgery and preprosthetic surgery

French: *Chirurgie des tissus mous et chirurgie préprothétique*.

Interventions to address selected anatomical features and prepare tissues for functional or prosthetic rehabilitation.

- Soft tissue grafts.
- Preprosthetic surgery, including removal of exostoses and vestibuloplasty.

This confirms the surgical preparation of tissues, not every subsequent prosthetic or cosmetic treatment.

### 5. Cysts and tumour-related conditions of the jaws

French: *Kystes et pathologies tumorales des mâchoires*.

- Diagnosis and surgical treatment of bony lesions of the jaws.

Do not broaden this into treatment of every head and neck tumour, a hospital oncology service or a guarantee of definitive treatment at the cabinet.

### 6. Salivary gland conditions

French: *Pathologies des glandes salivaires*.

Assessment and management of **certain** salivary gland conditions, including:

- Salivary stones.
- Mucoceles.
- Lesions of the minor salivary glands (*glandes salivaires accessoires*).

Keep the limitation to selected conditions; do not claim all salivary gland diseases or all forms of gland surgery.

### 7. Temporomandibular joint dysfunction

French: *Dysfonction des articulations temporo-mandibulaires (ATM)*.

- Diagnosis and management of temporomandibular joint disorders and associated orofacial pain.

The source does not specify a treatment technique. Do not invent joint surgery, injections, splints, physiotherapy or guaranteed pain relief.
