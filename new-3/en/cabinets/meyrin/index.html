    What to know before you come

    Access
    The practice is on the first floor of a building linked to imad, with no steps to climb. Access for people with reduced mobility is made easier. The Parking des Sports is located under the practice, with an entrance on Avenue Louis-Rendu. The Parking des Vergers is a few minutes' walk away.
    Diagnosis
    The practitioner examines first, then suggests an X-ray only when it adds information the examination alone does not give. The reason for the examination is explained to you.
    Hygiene
    Instruments go through a dedicated sterilisation room between two patients. [HYGIENE PROTOCOL TO BE CONFIRMED]
    Equipment
    X-rays are suggested when they add information needed for the diagnosis. [EQUIPMENT TO BE CONFIRMED]
    Languages
    French, English, German, Italian, Spanish and Portuguese, depending on who receives you.
