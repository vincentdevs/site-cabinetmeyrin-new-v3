    A first appointment at the dentist

    Booking is easier when you know how the consultation goes. Here are the steps, from booking to the written estimate. You can ask questions at every step, and no treatment starts without your agreement.

    Book an appointmentCall the practice
    1. 01

      Booking

      Online or by phone

      Book in the online diary at any hour, or call the practice Monday to Friday, 8am to 6.30pm. You do not need to know the name of the treatment. Just say what brings you and the team directs you to the right person.

    2. 02

      Arrival

      First floor, no steps

      The practice is at Place de la Diversité 1 in Meyrin. It is on the first floor of a building with no steps to climb. The « Meyrin, Hôpital de la Tour » stop is next door, and the Parking des Sports sits under the building.

    3. 03

      What to bring

      What is useful to us

      Bring an identity document, the list of your medicines if you take any and your recent X-rays if you have some. If you hold a supplementary dental insurance, bring its name too. If you do not have these documents, come anyway.

    4. 04

      First conversation

      We start by listening

      The practitioner asks what brings you, since when, and what you have already noticed. Your medical history and any worries are part of the conversation, because they change how the appointment is run.

    5. 05

      Examination

      Teeth, gums, restorations

      The practitioner examines the teeth, old restorations, gums, deposits and, when it concerns your situation, how the teeth meet. A check-up can spot a change before it causes discomfort.

    6. 06

      X-rays

      Only when they are useful

      X-rays are not systematic. They are suggested when they show something the examination alone cannot see, for instance between two teeth or under an old repair. The reason for the image is explained to you.

    7. 07

      Diagnosis

      What was found

      The practitioner tells you what was observed, what is fine and what needs care or monitoring. Sometimes no treatment is needed at all.

    8. 08

      Explanation

      In plain words

      Every observation is explained to you before any treatment is proposed. If a term is unclear, feel free to ask.

    9. 09

      Treatment options

      When several solutions exist

      When a treatment can be done in several ways, each option is presented with its steps, duration, limits and follow-up. You do not have to choose on the spot.

    10. 10

      Estimate

      Before you commit

      When a treatment is proposed, you can ask for a written estimate before starting. It lists the planned procedures and gives you time to decide.

    11. 11

      Next steps

      You decide

      Depending on the case, the appointment ends with no treatment, with monitoring, with a hygiene appointment or with a treatment to plan. What comes next is decided with you.

    What to bring

    • An identity document
    • The list of your medicines, if you take any
    • Your recent X-rays, if you have some
    • The name of your supplementary dental insurance, if you hold one

    If you do not have these documents, come anyway.

    If you wish, you can fill in the patient form online before you come.

    You can ask for a pause, an explanation or time to think at any moment

    If you feel anxious, say so on the phone or when you arrive, there is no need to explain why. We go at your pace, and a first check-up can quite simply end with no treatment.
