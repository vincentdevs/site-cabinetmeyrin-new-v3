    Edouard Di Donna

    Swiss federal specialist in oral surgery. Practice exclusively dedicated to oral surgery and stomatology.

    Book an appointmentCall the practice

    Dr Edouard Di Donna, a Swiss federal specialist in oral surgery, dedicates his practice exclusively to oral surgery and stomatology at Cabinet Dentaire Meyrin. He speaks French, Italian and English.

    Edouard Di Donna graduated from the Faculty of Medicine of the University of Geneva. The practice states that he directed his work towards oral surgery and implantology. He did so within the department of oral and maxillofacial surgery of the Geneva University Hospitals.

    He worked there as a resident dentist and then as chief resident until 2022. He diagnoses and treats conditions affecting the teeth, oral mucosa, jaws and associated tissues, after a thorough clinical assessment and in coordination with the other practitioners and referring doctors.
