    Welcome to the dental practice where we treat you the way we’d like to be treated

    In Geneva and Nyon, your dentist starts by listening, explains what they saw and sets out the options before any treatment. You decide what happens next, and the same team then looks after you from one check-up to the next, from prevention to implants.

    One team for all your dental care

    The practice brings together general dentistry, oral surgery, stomatology, implantology and dental hygiene. Dr Edouard Di Donna works exclusively in oral surgery and stomatology, in coordination with the other practitioners. From the check-up to implant placement, your care takes place in one location whenever possible. We examine you and explain what we have seen before we suggest any treatment.

    How we work

    Explaining before acting
    Every observation is explained and every step is announced. You know what is going to be done before it starts.
    Complete care, from the check-up to the implant
    General dentistry, oral surgery, implantology and dental hygiene are all carried out on site by the practice team whenever possible. You do not have to change address mid-treatment.
    X-rays only when they are useful
    They are suggested when they add information the clinical examination alone does not give. The reason is explained before the image is taken.
    Step-free access
    The Meyrin practice is on the first floor of a building with no steps to climb. It is at the Meyrin, Hôpital de la Tour stop. If you have a specific need, say so when you book.

    A first appointment at the dentist

    1. 01

      You tell us what brings you

      The reason for your visit, your medical history and what worries you. Nothing is decided at this stage.

    2. 02

      The practitioner examines you

      They look at the teeth, the gums and old restorations. An X-ray is only suggested when it adds information that is needed.

    3. 03

      They explain what they saw

      They use plain words and, when useful, the images of your own mouth. You can ask every question you have.

    4. 04

      They present the options

      When several solutions exist, each one is presented to you with its steps, its duration and its limits.

    5. 05

      You decide what happens next

      You have all the information in hand, and you can ask for a written estimate when a treatment is proposed.
