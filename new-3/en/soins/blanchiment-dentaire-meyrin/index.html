    Teeth whitening in Meyrin, assessment and result

    Before whitening, the dentist checks the teeth, the gums, any sensitivity and the visible restorations. The treatment changes the shade of natural teeth, but not that of crowns, composites or veneers. This assessment is used to plan the result you can expect and the protocol that suits your teeth.

    Book an appointmentCall the practice

    Why start with an assessment?

    A difference in shade can come from surface staining, the natural shade, a restoration or a single tooth. The assessment identifies what can respond to whitening and what needs another approach.

    Cavities, gum problems and sensitivity are taken into account before treatment. A hygiene appointment may be advised if deposits mask the shade.

    What result can be expected?

    The response varies with the initial shade, the nature of the staining and the teeth. The dentist explains the change the treatment can reasonably aim for, without promising the same shade for everyone.

    Crowns, veneers and composite restorations do not lighten like natural teeth. Their presence can influence the order of treatments.

    How is sensitivity taken into account?

    Temporary sensitivity can appear during or after whitening. The protocol, the duration and the instructions are adapted to the situation and to what you feel.

    If a tooth is already sensitive before treatment, its cause has to be looked for. Whitening does not replace the treatment of a painful tooth.

    Considering whitening?

    The assessment checks the teeth and explains the possible result.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is scaling enough to lighten the teeth?

    It removes deposits and some surface staining, but does not change the natural shade the way whitening does.

    Is the result permanent?

    The shade can change over time and with habits. Follow-up depends on the treatment and the situation.

    Can a single tooth be whitened?

    An isolated tooth of a different colour first needs a diagnosis. The treatment depends on the cause and on previous care.

    Do you have a question about this treatment?

    The appointment is there to examine your situation and explain what is possible. You decide afterwards.
