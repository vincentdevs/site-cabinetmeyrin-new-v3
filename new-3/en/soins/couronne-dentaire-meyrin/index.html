    Dental crown in Meyrin, indications and steps

    A crown covers the visible part of a tooth to give it back its shape. It also protects the tooth when it is very weakened. The dentist looks at what remains of the tooth, its position and the forces it bears before advising you.

    Book an appointmentCall the practice

    In which situations is a crown discussed?

    It may be proposed after extensive decay, a fracture, a large old repair or a root canal treatment. It is not necessary in every case.

    The dentist looks at what remains of the tooth, the gums, the root and the forces exerted when chewing. They first check that the tooth can be kept in good conditions.

    How is it made?

    The tooth is prepared according to the plan, then an impression or a scan is taken to make the crown. A temporary solution can be planned between the steps. The final crown is then checked and fixed.

    The number of appointments and the material depend on the situation. The practice explains the proposed choice and its maintenance before treatment.

    Which alternatives can be considered?

    When enough healthy tooth remains, a more limited restoration can sometimes be suitable. Conversely, if the tooth cannot be kept, the plan may have to address its extraction and replacement.

    The comparison covers the amount of tissue to preserve, the expected strength, access for cleaning and cost. The choice follows the examination, not a rule that is identical for every tooth.

    Has a crown been mentioned?

    The appointment examines the tooth and compares the possible restorations.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is a crown a false tooth?

    It covers a tooth that is kept. On an implant, by contrast, a crown replaces the visible part of a missing tooth.

    How long does a crown last?

    Its lifespan depends on the tooth, the forces, hygiene and follow-up. An individual figure cannot be promised before treatment.

    Can you get a cavity under a crown?

    The edge of the restoration and the root still need watching. Brushing, cleaning between the teeth and check-ups remain necessary.

    Do you have a question about this treatment?

    The appointment is there to examine your situation and explain what is possible. You decide afterwards.
