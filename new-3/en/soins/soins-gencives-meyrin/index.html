    Gum care in Meyrin, bleeding and follow-up

    Repeated bleeding can go with gum inflammation, often when plaque or tartar is present. Simply avoiding the area is not the answer. A check looks for the cause, cleans the deposits if needed and shows you the right technique.

    Book an appointmentCall the practice

    Which signs deserve a check?

    Frequent bleeding, redness, swelling, sensitivity, persistent bad breath or a change around a tooth can justify an appointment. Pain is not always present.

    These signs do not have a single cause. The check distinguishes a local irritation, an inflammation linked to deposits and other situations that need different care.

    How are the gums examined?

    The professional looks at the colour, the outline, the deposits and the bleeding. Certain areas around the teeth may be measured, and further examinations requested when they provide useful information.

    They also check your hygiene routine and the factors that make some areas hard to clean. The explanation starts from what is really observed in your mouth.

    How do the treatment and follow-up go?

    The treatment may combine professional cleaning and a daily routine adapted to the areas concerned. Depending on the extent, several appointments or a check after the initial phase may be proposed.

    The follow-up checks how the gums respond and adjusts the frequency of appointments. An improvement does not remove the need for daily care.

    Do your gums bleed regularly?

    The appointment is there to find the cause and suggest the right care.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is a bleeding gum necessarily diseased?

    Bleeding can go with inflammation, but an examination is needed to understand its cause.

    Is a mouthwash enough?

    It does not remove tartar and does not replace brushing or cleaning between the teeth. The team tells you whether it can help in your situation.

    How often should I come back?

    The frequency depends on the state of your gums and how they respond to treatment.

    Do you have a question about this treatment?

    The appointment is there to examine your situation and explain what is possible. You decide afterwards.
