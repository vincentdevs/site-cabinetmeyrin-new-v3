    A missing tooth, implant, bridge or denture

    A missing tooth can be replaced by an implant with a crown, a bridge or a removable prosthesis. The choice depends on the position of the tooth, the state of the neighbouring teeth, the gums, the bone and your priorities. An examination compares these solutions before deciding.

    Book an appointmentCall the practice

    What can a missing tooth change?

    The consequences vary with its position and the way the other teeth meet. A missing tooth can hinder chewing or let the neighbouring teeth drift. In other situations, the inconvenience stays limited.

    The dentist looks at the space, the neighbouring teeth and the gums. They also tell you whether the area can simply be monitored without replacing the tooth straight away.

    How to compare implant, bridge and removable prosthesis?

    The implant replaces a support in the bone and can hold a crown. The bridge generally rests on the neighbouring teeth. The removable prosthesis is taken out for cleaning and can replace one or several teeth. Each solution requires different conditions.

    The comparison covers the available tissues, the teeth that could serve as supports, the course of treatment, the maintenance and the cost. No solution is preferable in every situation.

    When should the replacement be prepared?

    If an extraction is planned, it can be useful to talk about the replacement before the procedure. Healing and the future tooth can then be thought through together. If the tooth has been missing for a long time, a consultation is still entirely possible.

    The timeline depends on the area and the chosen treatment. The dentist explains the steps and gives you an estimate after the examination.

    Would you like to replace a tooth?

    The appointment examines the area and compares the possible solutions.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does a missing tooth always need replacing?

    No. The decision depends on the tooth, its function and the risk of drifting. An examination compares replacement and monitoring.

    Can I choose without an X-ray?

    The examination starts with the mouth. The dentist then indicates the imaging needed depending on the solution being studied.

    Is the price the same for each solution?

    No. It depends on the chosen treatment and the associated care. An individual estimate is drawn up after the assessment.

    Do you have a question about this treatment?

    The appointment is there to examine your situation and explain what is possible. You decide afterwards.
