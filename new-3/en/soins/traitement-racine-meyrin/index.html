    Root canal in Meyrin, steps and what follows

    This treatment may be proposed when the living part inside a tooth is affected but the tooth can still be kept. The dentist cleans and disinfects the small canals inside the roots, then seals them. Each step is explained before it starts.

    Book an appointmentCall the practice

    Why does the inside of a tooth need treating?

    Deep decay, a crack or a knock can reach the living part at the centre of the tooth. The signs vary, and the tooth does not always hurt. The examination is there to understand the cause and check whether the tooth can still be kept.

    The aim of the treatment is to keep your tooth whenever that remains possible. If it is too weakened, the dentist explains the other options.

    How does a root canal treatment go?

    The dentist opens an access to the inside of the tooth, removes the affected part, cleans the canals and seals them. Depending on the tooth and the difficulty of the treatment, one or several appointments may be needed. If a highly specialised treatment is preferable, the dentist explains this and refers you to a colleague.

    Why does the tooth need restoring afterwards?

    A treated tooth may have lost a significant part of its structure. A filling, a rebuild or a crown may be proposed to protect it, depending on the tooth and what remains of it. This step is part of the plan explained before the end of the treatment.

    Is a tooth hurting?

    An examination looks for the cause and checks whether the tooth can be kept.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is a root canal done in one session?

    Sometimes, but several sessions may be needed depending on the tooth, its anatomy and the situation.

    Does the pain disappear immediately?

    How things evolve depends on the cause and the treatment carried out. The practice tells you what to expect and when to call back.

    Is a crown needed after the treatment?

    Not always. The restoration depends on the tooth and how much structure remains.

    Do you have a question about this treatment?

    The appointment is there to examine your situation and explain what is possible. You decide afterwards.
