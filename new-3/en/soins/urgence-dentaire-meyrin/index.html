    Dental emergency in Meyrin, what to do

    Severe pain, a broken tooth, swelling or a dental injury need to be assessed. Call Cabinet Dentaire Meyrin on +41 22 320 19 19 and describe the symptoms, when they started and how they are evolving. The team will tell you what to do based on what you describe.

    Book an appointmentCall the practice

    When should you call the practice?

    Contact the practice when pain becomes hard to bear or when a tooth breaks. Do the same when swelling appears or when a knock affects a tooth. The degree of urgency cannot be determined from a web page. It depends on the symptoms, the examination and sometimes an X-ray.

    Difficulty breathing, loss of consciousness or uncontrollable bleeding are matters for the emergency services. In Switzerland, call 144 immediately.

    What to do while waiting for the appointment?

    Avoid applying any product directly to the tooth or gum without professional advice. In case of bleeding after a procedure or a knock, follow the instructions received from the practice. If symptoms change or get worse, call again so the situation can be reassessed.

    For an injury, say on the phone when the knock happened. Also say whether a tooth moved, broke or came out of its socket. These details help the team prepare the consultation.

    How does an emergency consultation go?

    The dentist starts by identifying the likely source of the problem and checking the tissues concerned. The first aim may be to relieve, stabilise the situation or protect the tooth. If further treatment is needed, the options and next steps are explained before deciding.

    An emergency therefore does not always lead to a complete treatment on the same day. What is done depends on the examination and on what can be done in good conditions.

    Frequently asked questions

    When should I call for a dental emergency?

    Call in case of severe pain, a broken tooth, swelling, injury or bleeding that worries you. Describe the symptoms precisely on the phone.

    Does the practice see patients without an appointment?

    Call before coming. The team can tell you when to come depending on the situation and availability.

    What should I do outside opening hours?

    Follow the instructions on the practice's answering machine or contact the on-call service of the SSO Geneva. In a life-threatening emergency, call 144.

    Do you have a question about this treatment?

    The appointment is there to examine your situation and explain what is possible. You decide afterwards.
