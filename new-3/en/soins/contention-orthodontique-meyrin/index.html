    Retainer after orthodontics in Meyrin, keeping the result

    After orthodontic treatment, the teeth tend to drift back towards their old position. A retainer holds them in place while the bone and gums settle. The practitioner explains which type suits you and how long to wear it.

    Book an appointmentCall the practice

    Which types of retainer?

    A thin wire can be bonded behind the front teeth, where it does not show. A clear tray worn at night is another option, sometimes combined with the wire.

    The choice depends on the movements made and on your habits. Both are presented to you with their advantages and constraints.

    How do you look after it?

    The wire is cleaned with the toothbrush and a suitable floss, which the team shows you. The tray is rinsed and brushed every day.

    Check-ups make sure the wire stays bonded and the teeth stay in place. If the wire comes loose, call the practice as soon as you notice.

    Frequently asked questions

    How long should a retainer be worn?

    Often for several years, and sometimes with no end date for a bonded wire. The practitioner tells you the length that suits your situation.

    What if the wire comes loose?

    Call the practice. The wire can be bonded again, and it is best done quickly so the teeth do not move.

    Do you have a question about this treatment?

    The appointment is there to examine your situation and explain what is possible. You decide afterwards.
