    Dental treatments provided in Meyrin and Nyon

    The treatments are grouped into four families, according to what brings you in. It can be a check-up, a tooth to treat, a tooth to replace or the look of your smile. Each page explains what the practitioner checks, how the treatment goes and what you will be able to decide after the examination. You do not need to know the name of the treatment to book.

    Book an appointmentCall the practice

    Prevent

    Check-ups, dental hygiene and gum health.

    A check-up can spot a cavity or an inflammation before it hurts. At the hygienist, scaling removes the tartar your toothbrush cannot, and gum follow-up helps stop an inflammation from settling in. Children come for a first check-up, at their own pace.

    Treat

    Cavities, root canal treatments and conservative care.

    An affected tooth is examined before it is treated. Depending on what the examination shows, care ranges from a filling to a root canal treatment. Oral surgery takes over for an extraction or a wisdom tooth. Pain that cannot wait is seen as an emergency.

    Restore

    Crowns, bridges and dental implants.

    A badly damaged tooth can be protected by a crown, a missing tooth replaced by an implant or a bridge. The practice's oral surgeon places implants on site, and the choice between solutions is explained before any decision.

    Smile aesthetics

    Whitening, orthodontics and night guards.

    Whitening acts on the shade of the teeth, orthodontics on their position. Clear aligners and fixed braces move the teeth, a retainer keeps the result and a night guard protects the teeth from grinding. An examination always comes before the decision.
