    Fixed braces in Meyrin, fitting and follow-up

    Fixed braces stay on the teeth for the whole treatment. Small brackets bonded to each tooth guide a wire that moves the teeth gradually. The practitioner explains why they suggest braces and what the treatment asks of you day to day.

    Book an appointmentCall the practice

    Which situations do they suit?

    Braces allow precise movements, including in more complex situations. They suit children, teenagers and adults, depending on what the examination shows.

    Brackets come in metal or in tooth-coloured ceramic. The choice between the two is presented to you with their differences.

    How does the treatment go?

    Fitting takes one appointment. The wire is then adjusted at regular check-ups, and some discomfort can be felt for a few days after each adjustment.

    Brushing needs more care, because the brackets hold on to plaque. The team shows you how to clean around them, and a hygiene appointment can be planned during treatment.

    And after the treatment?

    When the teeth are in place, the braces are removed and a retainer helps keep the result. It is part of the treatment and is explained to you before removal.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is fitting painful?

    The fitting itself does not hurt. Some sensitivity can appear for a few days after fitting and after each adjustment, then it fades.

    Can I eat normally?

    Most foods, yes. Very hard or sticky foods can loosen a bracket and are best avoided.

    Can an adult wear braces?

    Yes. Age matters less than the health of the teeth and gums, which the examination checks.

    Do you have a question about this treatment?

    The appointment is there to examine your situation and explain what is possible. You decide afterwards.
