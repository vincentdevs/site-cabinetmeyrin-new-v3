    Tooth extraction in Meyrin, aftercare and follow-up

    An extraction is considered when a tooth cannot be kept or when it causes a problem that cannot be treated otherwise. The dentist first checks the possibilities of keeping it, explains the procedure and prepares the aftermath, including replacement if indicated.

    Book an appointmentCall the practice

    Why might a tooth need removing?

    Very extensive decay, a fracture, an infection, a gum problem or a poor position can lead to discussing an extraction. Before proposing it, the dentist always checks whether the tooth can still be treated and kept.

    They explain what can be kept and the consequences of each choice. A wisdom tooth or a more delicate extraction may need a specific examination.

    What should be planned before the procedure?

    The assessment identifies the tooth concerned, its shape, the neighbouring tissues and the necessary examinations. Mention your illnesses, medicines, allergies and any recent change in your health. Do not change a prescribed treatment without the agreement of the professional who follows it.

    The anaesthesia, the course of the procedure and the instructions afterwards are explained before the appointment. You can ask your questions before giving your consent.

    What is the aftermath like?

    The practice gives instructions adapted to the procedure carried out. They cover in particular protecting the area, food, hygiene and taking the prescribed medicines. The aftermath is not the same for every extraction.

    The signs that should lead you to call back are detailed. Difficulty breathing, loss of consciousness or uncontrollable bleeding require calling 144. Any replacement of the tooth is planned according to healing.

    Has an extraction been mentioned?

    The appointment checks the indication and prepares the aftermath.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does a painful tooth necessarily need removing?

    No. Several treatments can keep it depending on the cause. The examination determines what is possible.

    Can an implant be prepared before the extraction?

    The future solution can be discussed before the procedure. Its timing however depends on the tooth, the tissues and the expected healing.

    What if the pain increases after the procedure?

    Follow the instructions given and contact the practice if the evolution does not match what was announced. Do the same if a warning sign appears.

    Do you have a question about this treatment?

    The appointment is there to examine your situation and explain what is possible. You decide afterwards.
