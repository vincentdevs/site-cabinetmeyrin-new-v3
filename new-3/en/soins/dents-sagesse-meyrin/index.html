    Wisdom teeth in Meyrin, when to come in

    A wisdom tooth does not always need to be extracted. A consultation is indicated when it causes pain, swelling, gum inflammation or when a check-up shows a problem of position or space. The decision to keep or extract rests on the clinical examination and, if needed, on an X-ray.

    Book an appointmentCall the practice

    Which signs should lead you to come in?

    A wisdom tooth can stay silent or cause inflammation of the gum covering it. Pain at the back of the mouth, swelling, discomfort when opening, a persistent bad taste or repeated episodes justify an assessment.

    In case of progressive swelling, fever or difficulty swallowing or breathing, contact a professional quickly. Difficulty breathing is a matter for 144.

    How is the decision to keep or extract made?

    The practitioner assesses the position of the tooth, how easily it can be cleaned and the space available. The state of the gum and the relationship of the tooth with neighbouring structures are also assessed. An X-ray may be needed to see what the examination cannot reach.

    A well positioned tooth with no problem can be monitored. An extraction may be discussed when symptoms, infections or local risks justify it.

    How is the extraction prepared?

    The preliminary appointment explains the procedure, the instructions and the expected aftermath. The practitioner takes your treatments, history and allergies into account. After the procedure, follow the instructions received and call the practice if the evolution worries you.

    Is a wisdom tooth bothering you?

    The consultation looks for the cause and decides what comes next.

    Frequently asked questions

    Do all four wisdom teeth need removing?

    Not necessarily. Each tooth is assessed according to its position and the problem observed.

    Can a wisdom tooth cause an emergency?

    Yes, inflammation or infection around the tooth can become painful. Call the practice to describe the symptoms.

    Can I work after an extraction?

    Getting back to work depends on the procedure, the aftermath and your activity. The practitioner gives you a suitable recommendation after the assessment.

    Do you have a question about this treatment?

    The appointment is there to examine your situation and explain what is possible. You decide afterwards.
