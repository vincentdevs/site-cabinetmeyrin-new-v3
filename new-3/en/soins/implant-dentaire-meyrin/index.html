    Dental implant in Meyrin, steps and alternatives

    A dental implant replaces the root of a missing tooth and can support a crown or a prosthesis. The decision depends on the tooth to replace, the state of the gums, the bone available and your general health. A consultation compares the implant with a bridge or a removable solution.

    Book an appointmentCall the practice

    When can an implant be considered?

    An implant can be studied when a tooth is missing or cannot be kept. The examination covers the area to treat, the neighbouring teeth, the gums and the bone volume. Health habits and certain conditions can also change the plan or the timeline.

    A gap therefore does not mean an implant is automatically indicated. The project has to take the whole mouth into account and the way the future tooth will be used.

    How does the treatment go?

    The first consultation clarifies the need and gathers the necessary examinations. If the implant is chosen, its placement is planned. A healing period may be needed before the prosthetic tooth is made. The number of appointments and the timeline vary with the clinical situation.

    Before starting, the practice explains the steps, the alternatives and the expected follow-up. An estimate depends on the chosen plan and cannot be given reliably without an examination.

    Implant or bridge: how to compare?

    An implant rests on the bone, whereas a bridge generally rests on neighbouring teeth. The bridge can make sense in some situations, in particular when the neighbouring teeth already need restoring. The implant can avoid using them as supports, but it requires a surgical assessment.

    The right choice therefore depends on the state of the neighbouring teeth, the bone and the gums. It also depends on the treatment time and the patient's preferences. The comparison is made on those criteria, not on a solution presented as better in every case.

    How to replace several teeth?

    Several missing teeth can be replaced by a fixed or removable prosthesis, sometimes supported by implants. The number of implants is not necessarily the same as the number of teeth replaced. The plan depends on the distribution of supports and the functional aim.

    A consultation separates what belongs to surgery from what belongs to prosthetics, then organises the steps in the right order.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can an implant be placed when bone is lacking?

    The quantity and quality of bone are assessed before the decision. Depending on the situation, bone preparation may be discussed or another solution proposed.

    Is placing an implant painful?

    The procedure and its aftermath are explained before the treatment. Perception varies with the procedure and the person, so the practitioner details the measures adapted to your situation.

    How much does a dental implant cost?

    The cost depends on the examinations, the surgery, the prosthesis and any additional procedures. An individual estimate is drawn up after the assessment.

    Do you have a question about this treatment?

    The appointment is there to examine your situation and explain what is possible. You decide afterwards.
